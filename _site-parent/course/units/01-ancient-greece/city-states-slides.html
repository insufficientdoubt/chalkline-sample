Mountains and sea

  • Greece is a rocky peninsulapeninsula 半岛Land with water on three sides.History Vocab ↗ with many islands.
  • Mountains split the land into small valleys.
  • Each valley grew its own city-statecity-state 城邦A city and the land around it, with its own government.History Vocab ↗: one city and its farms.
  • The sea connected them. Greeks traded by ship and started a colonycolony 殖民地A new town started by people from another city.History Vocab ↗ wherever they found good land.

Key words: who decides?

city-state 城邦

A city and the land around it, with its own government.

citizen 公民

A member of a city-state with rights, like the right to vote.

democracy 民主

Government where citizens make decisions by voting.

assembly 公民大会

A meeting where citizens discuss and vote on laws.

How democracy worked in Athens

  • The assemblyassembly 公民大会A meeting where citizens discuss and vote on laws.History Vocab ↗ met about 40 times a year on a hill called the Pnyx.
  • Any citizencitizen 公民A member of a city-state with rights, like the right to vote.History Vocab ↗ could stand up and speak.
  • Then they voted, usually by raising their hands: votevote 投票To choose officially, for example by raising your hand.History Vocab ↗.
  • In court, juries of hundreds of citizens voted with small tokens.

Demos = people, kratos = power. Democracy = power of the people.

Who could vote?

Could vote

Free adult men with Athenian parents

Could not vote

  • Women
  • Enslaved people
  • Foreigners living in Athens
