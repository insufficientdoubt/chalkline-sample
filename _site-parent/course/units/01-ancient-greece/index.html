Unit 1: Ancient Greece

How geography shaped the Greek city-states, how democracy worked in Athens, and the Greek ideas we still use today.
In this unit
  • I can explain how mountains and the sea led to many separate city-states.
  • I can compare life and government in Athens and Sparta.
  • I can describe how Athenian democracy worked, and who could and couldn’t vote.
  • I can name Greek ideas we still use today, from the Olympics to columns and theater.
